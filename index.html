Still Lit

Still Lit begins with a collection of lamps found across the city. Each lamp carries a different form, color, and way of giving light, but its meaning extends beyond the object itself. A light can suggest that someone is home, invite us to stop, mark the passing of time, or quietly change how a place feels. Switch on the page and explore the floating lamps: some transform the background through color, while others reveal fragments of writing about design, space, and belonging. Together, they ask us to notice how light becomes part of everyday life—and what it means for something to remain lit.
